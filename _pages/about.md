I am a Computer Science student at **Penn State** graduating in **May 2027**, focused on building reliable AI systems at the intersection of **LLM agents, multimodal machine learning, model evaluation, computer vision, and AI infrastructure**.

My research experience includes multimodal behavior analysis over **1,500+ hours of parent-child interaction video**, eye-tracking and facial-expression analysis for student engagement, and evaluation-oriented machine learning. I am also a co-author of a 2025 IISE Conference and Expo publication on measuring student attentiveness with eye-tracking and visual-spatial analytics.

I like projects where the model is only one part of the system: data pipelines, evaluation harnesses, tool-using agents, local inference, APIs, containers, storage, observability, and the engineering needed to make an AI product actually work.
