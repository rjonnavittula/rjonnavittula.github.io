--- layout: archive title: "Projects" permalink: /portfolio/ author_profile: false --- I build AI systems that combine models with the infrastructure around them: evaluation, agents, document pipelines, APIs, self-hosting, and multimodal machine learning. ## Selected projects ### HIVE Scholar A self-hosted planning and learning backend built with FastAPI, Postgres, Docker, API-key authentication, LMS ingestion, and deadline-aware scheduling logic. The project is designed as an automation-ready backend for student productivity and learning workflows. [View HIVE Scholar on GitHub](https://github.com/rjonnavittula/hive-scholar) ### InjecAgent — Local Model & Evaluation Experiments Experiments around indirect prompt-injection attacks against tool-integrated LLM agents, including local-model runs and parallel evaluation workflows. This work builds on the InjecAgent benchmark and focuses on practical agent-security evaluation rather than claiming authorship of the original benchmark. [View the experiments on GitHub](https://github.com/rjonnavittula/InjecAgent) ### Forge — Self-Hosted AI Infrastructure A self-hosted LLM environment built around Ollama, Proxmox, Docker/LXC, GPU acceleration, networking, and ZFS-backed persistent storage. The goal is to make local model serving reliable enough to support real applications and experimentation. ### WatchBot — Computer Vision for Student Engagement An early computer-vision system for analyzing student engagement during online learning. The project used OpenCV, facial analysis, and a CNN-based mood classifier with real-time lecture monitoring. ## Research My research has included multimodal behavior analysis, model training and evaluation, eye-tracking, facial-expression analysis, and visual-spatial analytics. I am a co-author of a 2025 IISE Conference and Expo publication on measuring student attentiveness using eye-tracking and visual-spatial data. ## What I want to build next I am especially interested in agent evaluation, local-model performance, multimodal document intelligence, AI infrastructure, and production systems where reliability and measurable quality matter as much as the model itself.
